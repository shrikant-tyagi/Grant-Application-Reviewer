APPLICATION: "Connect Riverside" Digital Skills Programme

Organisation
Bright Futures Community Trust is a registered nonprofit (registration number NP-48213) founded in 2021. We are the largest digital inclusion provider in the state.

Project summary
We request $48,000 to run a 12-month digital skills programme starting in November 2026. The programme will serve residents across the region, with a focus on older adults and job seekers.

Outcomes
We aim to improve digital literacy in our community. We will also train 200 residents to complete the "Basic Online Skills" certificate by the end of the project. Our 2025 pilot achieved 98% participant satisfaction.

Budget
Staff and tutors: $30,400. Equipment and venue: $8,000. Administration and overheads: $9,600. Total request: $48,000. Our itemised budget is attached.

Partnerships
We have a confirmed partnership with Riverside Public Library, who will host the sessions.

Financial position
Our accounts are available on request.

Sustainability
We hope to continue the programme using future grants and donations.
