RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY DIGITAL SKILLS FUND 2026/27
Applicant Guidelines

1. Purpose
The Fund supports projects that improve digital skills among residents of Riverside County.

2. Eligibility
2.1 Applicants must be a registered nonprofit organisation and must provide their registration number.
2.2 Applicants must have been operating for at least two years at the date of application.
2.3 The project must primarily benefit residents of Riverside County.

3. Funding
3.1 Applicants may request between $10,000 and $50,000.
3.2 Administrative costs must not exceed 15% of the total request.
3.3 Applicants are encouraged to provide match funding of at least 10% of the project cost.

4. Project requirements
4.1 Projects must start no earlier than 1 January 2027 and must not run longer than 12 months.
4.2 Applications must include at least two measurable outcomes.
4.3 Applicants should describe how the project will be sustained after funding ends.

5. Submission requirements
5.1 Applicants must submit independently audited or examined accounts for the most recent financial year.
5.2 Applicants must submit a board resolution authorising the application.
5.3 Applicants must submit an itemised project budget.
5.4 Applicants should include at least one letter of support from a project partner.
5.5 The narrative must not exceed 1,500 words.
